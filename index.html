Our Core Mission & Vision

Life Line Hospital Ambikapur was established to bridge the critical healthcare gap in Northern Chhattisgarh (Surguja, Surajpur, Balrampur, Koriya & Jashpur districts), providing 24x7 emergency medical attention, cardiac interventions, and advanced surgical capabilities.

We believe every patient deserves empathetic, transparent, and international-standard medical treatment. Our facility features 150+ inpatient beds, 25 ICU beds, laminar airflow operation theatres, and round-the-clock resident medical officers.

🎯 Our Vision

To be the region's most trusted hospital for emergency response and specialized clinical treatment.

❤️ Our Mission

Affordable, high-quality tertiary medical care delivered with zero-compromise patient safety.

Accreditations, Ayushman & TPA Empanelment

Life Line Hospital Ambikapur adheres to NABH compliant clinical quality standards and is empaneled with Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY & 15+ leading health insurance TPAs for 100% cashless medical treatment.

Government Cashless Scheme

How to Claim Cashless Admission (Step-by-Step)

01

TPA Desk Visit

Present your Ayushman Card / Insurance Card & Government ID at Ground Floor TPA Desk.

02

Pre-Auth Request

Hospital TPA Desk fills pre-authorization form and submits to insurance team.

03

Cashless Approval

Receive instant pre-authorization approval for treatment without upfront payment.

04

Direct Settlement

Hospital settles hospital bill directly with TPA at discharge.
