Our Vision & Mission

Sanjeevani Hospital Ambikapur was founded in 2012 to address the critical need for round-the-clock emergency medical services, cardiac care, and advanced surgical procedures in Northern Chhattisgarh.

We believe every patient deserves empathetic, transparent, and international-standard medical care. Our hospital is equipped with 200+ beds, 25 ICU beds, laminar airflow operation theatres, and round-the-clock specialists.
